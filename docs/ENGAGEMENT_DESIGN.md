# BeyForge — engagement plan (picked 2026-11-02)

The goal is a full game people keep coming back to (fairly addictive), built on research into TCG Pocket, Clash Royale, Brawl Stars, retention benchmarks and Play Store rules.
**Everything here must be tunable from BeyForge Studio** (amounts, timers, odds, schedules, on/off), so devs never touch code.
The owner also asked for better overall visuals and animations everywhere.

## Picked features (all 20)

### Daily habit
1. **Instant first battle**
   - A new player gets a scripted battle with a starter Bey in about 60 seconds.
   - They then open a free starter pack, and are asked to sign up only after that.
   - Early levels (1–5) come fast.
2. **Free pack timer**
   - A free pack every 12h, banking up to 2.
   - Hourglasses cut the wait.
   - "Pack ready" phone notifications are local (Capacitor), so they work on the free plan.
3. **Streak overhaul**
   - A 30-day reward calendar with a big day-7 jackpot.
   - Streak freezes protect a missed day.
4. **First win of the day:** big XP, coins and a mini chest.

### Collecting & progression
5. **Beydex:** every part, shown as a silhouette until owned. It tracks completion %, gives set rewards and lists shiny variants. Pack reveals show "NEW · 48/312".
6. **Forge Shards:** duplicates become shards. Shards level up or craft the exact part you want.
7. **Evolutions:** a max-level part evolves into a new 3D look with a stronger signature move.
8. **Masteries:** win games with a part to earn mastery ranks, titles, frames and badges.

### Every match
9. **Reward cascade:** the results screen fills the XP, part XP, mission and pass bars one by one, with sounds and level-up pops.
10. **Spin Drops:** a tap-to-open reward after wins that can upgrade its rarity mid-animation. Capped per day.
11. **Finisher replays:** a slow-mo replay of the winning hit with an announcer, savable or shareable as a clip/GIF.
12. **In-battle bounties:** bonus goals each match, such as "win by burst" or "land 3 moves".

### Social
13. **Ghost battles:** fight other players' real Beys run by the AI. The owner gets notified when their Bey is beaten.
14. **Steal a part:** pick 1 of 5 face-down parts from a friend's recent pack. The friend gets coins.
15. **Weekly leagues:** 30-player groups each week, with promotion, relegation and rewards.
16. **Showcase likes:** likes on showcased Beys and parts earn tickets. Studio runs a "Bey of the Week" contest.

### Seasons & live
17. **Monthly seasons + free pass:** a new element theme each month, a free reward track, a ranked season and season-exclusive parts. A premium track comes later.
18. **Event modes:** Boss Rush, King of the Stadium, Mirror Match and Chaos Stadium, scheduled from Studio.
19. **Weekly tournaments:** brackets with entry tickets, live spectating and trophy rewards.
20. **Quest chains + mystery chest:** story-style quest lines that end in a big chest.

## Guardrails (from the research)
- Odds are always shown for random rewards. This is required on Google Play.
- When real money arrives, sell gems, the pass and cosmetics, not paid random packs. Some countries ban paid loot boxes.
- No manipulative purchase pressure. Chat needs a safety reminder and parent controls if kids are a target audience.
- Prefer "comeback" mechanics such as streak freezes and event reruns over harsh FOMO.

## Retention targets to watch in Studio
- **D1:** median ~22%; top quartile 25–33%.
- **D7:** median ~3.5–4%; top quartile 7–8%.
- It takes roughly 900 installs to read D1 within ±3 points.
