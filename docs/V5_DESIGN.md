# BeyForge v5 — gameplay design (from Aiden's 40 answers, 2026-11-01)

## Core feel
- Skill is in **building and launching**. In the fight the Bey moves on its own; the player gets a light **tap-to-lean** nudge (tap a spot, Bey drifts that way a bit) and taps moves.
- Matches are **quick (2–3 min)**. Outcome = design + launch + **some luck / upsets**.

## Launch
- Inputs: **aim position** (where you launch from), **tilt/angle**, existing power timing meter.
- **Named shots**: Flat Shot, Bank Shot, Sliding Shoot, Power Shoot (each changes the opening).
- Bad launch = **moderate** penalty. Launches **never fail completely**.

## Moves ("launch formations", Pokémon-style)
- Each part slot has **up to 3 moves**: Blade/Layer, Core (Ratchet/Disc), Driver/Bit. You pick 1 per part **every launch** (mind games).
- Move pool comes from the part's **shape/settings**; moves 2 and 3 unlock by **leveling the part** (5.1).
- Moves fire with a **tap button** during the round. **Cooldown:** you can't use the same move two rounds in a row.
- **Legendary** parts/drivers have **signature moves**.

## Elements
- 10 elements: Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Lightning, Ice, Light, Dark, Metal, Void.
- Comes from a **mix of parts** (each part leans toward an element) and the player **picks in the Forge** from what the parts allow.
- **Dual typing** like Pokémon. Advantage is about **25%**.
- Elements change move effects and cause **status effects** (Burn = spin drain, Freeze = slow, Shock = wobble), plus element visuals and stadium terrain.

## Collection and progression (5.1)
- **Own everything:** you must own a copy of a part to battle with it. Everything stays 3D-printable.
- **IVs:** each part copy rolls fixed random IVs. **Regular players can never edit IVs in any way** (no rerolls, no items). Only the owner, or an admin role with that permission, can change them.
- **EVs:** players earn points by leveling the part and assign them themselves, so regular players can edit these.
- **Owned copies:** paint can be changed, the shape can't (shape is locked per copy).
- **Skill trees:** per part, plus an account-wide blader tree. Leveling gives moves 2/3, small stat boosts, cosmetic tiers (bronze/silver/gold glow) and move upgrades (Fang Rush → II).
- **Getting parts:** win drops, booster packs (coins), crafting/minting with coins, and trading part copies with friends.
- **Rarity:** Common/Rare/Epic/Legendary plus **Shiny** variants of any part. **Limited** = achievement parts, which are separate from Legendary.
- **Legendaries** come from rare drops, the shop (expensive) and boss battles. Limited parts come from achievements.
- **Starters:** pick 1 of 3 starter Beys (Attack/Defense/Stamina) plus a box of commons.
- **Parts tab** (collection box) plus a quick picker inside the Forge.
- **Ranked:** the owner decides whether IV/EV/levels count (toggle in the Ranked rules editor).

## Modes and content (5.2)
- **Bosses:** story/tower ladder, weekly boss (owner sets it), element gyms with badges, co-op raid with a friend.
- **Terrain stadiums:** Lava (Fire), Ice rink (Ice/Water), Storm (Lightning/Wind), Void/Temple (Dark/Light).
- **CPU difficulty:** Easy/Normal/Hard/Expert, and story mode scales with the player.

## Rollout
- **5.0:** launch formations (aim, tilt, named shots), part move pools, move buttons with cooldown, elements with dual types, matchups, status effects and visuals, tap-to-lean, CPU difficulty.
- **5.1:** part ownership, IVs/EVs, part levels and skill trees, blader tree, packs, drops, crafting, rarities and shinies, starters, Parts tab, part trading.
- **5.2:** bosses, tower, gyms, raids, weekly boss, terrain stadiums, legendary signature moves.
