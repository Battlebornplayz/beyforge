# BeyForge UI overhaul: design answers (2026-11-02)

**Base style:** hybrid of the X-treme look (black/yellow, slanted cuts, condensed type) and Neon Arena (glass, cyan glow, radar).

## Overall
- **Background:** different per tab (Forge = workshop/pedestal, Battle = arena, Shop = store, etc.)
- **Accent color:** a Settings option (default hybrid yellow + cyan)
- **Shapes:** a mix. Buttons and tabs are slanted; panels are glass with one cut corner (Q17).
- **Glow:** subtle, on highlights only
- **Headings:** techy (Chakra Petch)
- **Navigation:**
  - PC: bottom dock with icons + text (dock icon style picked from 5 options)
  - Phone: bottom tab bar with icons
  - Icons + text throughout (the app is going to the Play Store)

## Forge
- Layout: controls on the left, stats on the right, Bey in the middle
- Part pickers: picture tiles
- Stats: radar chart
- Stage: glowing pedestal behind the Bey

## Battle
- **HUD:** fighting-game style, with big slanted bars from each side and portraits
- **Launch:** full launcher view (close-up of the Bey on the launcher plus a ripcord animation)
- **Pre-match:** VS splash, then the camera sweep
- **Results:** stats breakdown (hits, bursts, moves used, damage) plus rewards

## Shop overhaul
- **Look:** game-store layout (featured banner, tabs, item grid)
- **Rotation:** daily items plus weekly featured
- **Sells:** cosmetics, packs & crates, specific parts, bundles
- **Currency:** coins plus **gems**. Gems come from achievements, weekly boss/raids, daily streaks and ranked season rewards. Never real money.
- **Pack opening:** super flashy, like Pokémon TCG Pocket / Clash Royale. Swipe to tear the pack, rarity build-up glow, holo shimmer, burst on each reveal.
- **Odds:** always shown
- **Pity:** pity counter plus duplicate protection
- **Extras:** wishlist, try-on preview, gift to friends, sales & events
- **Control:** rotates automatically, and the owner can override (pin featured items, set prices, run sales) in Admin

## Home and profile
- **Home hub on open:** Bey on display, daily rewards, quick Battle/Forge buttons, news
- **Splash:** rip animation (ripcord pull, logo launches in)
- **Profile:** player card. You can customize banners, titles, avatar frames and a showcase Bey.

## Motion and feel
- Page transitions: slide
- Buttons: squish press plus a light sweep
- Idle motion: lots ("constant dopamine"): particles, spinning Beys, shimmering cards
- UI sounds: punchy (clicks, whooshes, coin dings, pack tears)
- Phone density: balanced by default, adjustable in Settings

## Settings
- **Themes:** Hybrid (default), plus unlockable themes (Metal, Anime, Burst, X-treme, Neon, ...)
- **Accessibility:** text size, reduce motion, colorblind mode, high contrast

## Play Store
- BeyForge brand only, with no official Beyblade names or logos in the listing
- App icon: pick from options
