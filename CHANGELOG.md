# BeyForge update log

## V6.6 — The Hook
- **Instant first battle:** new players pick a starter Bey, fight right away, then rip open a free starter pack before being asked to sign up.
- **Free pack timer:** a free pack every 12 hours, saving up to 2. Hourglasses skip the wait. On Android, your phone pings you when a pack is ready.
- **30-day streak calendar:**
  - Jackpot days on 7, 14, 21, 28 and 30.
  - 🧊 Streak freezes save your streak if you miss a day.
- **First win of the day:** bonus XP, coins and a free Spin Drop.
- **Spin Drops:** after wins, open a capsule that can upgrade from Common all the way to Legendary mid-animation.
- **Reward cascade:** the results screen now fills your XP bar (with level-up pops), counts coins, and shows missions, your free pack timer and Spin Drops.
- Finishing all 3 daily missions gives an hourglass.
- Staff badges show their custom text in the game.
- Ranks are now managed in BeyForge Studio.

## V6.5 — BeyForge Studio
- New **BeyForge Studio** owner dashboard (PC app + web):
  - Analytics and player management.
  - A no-code content creator for packs, parts and cosmetics, with a live 3D preview.
  - Publishing with drafts, review, scheduling and % rollout.
  - Game switches, staff ranks and an audit log with undo.
- The game now picks up Studio content live:
  - Custom packs, parts and cosmetics.
  - Maintenance mode, feature toggles and forced updates.
  - 2× coin/XP/drop events with a banner.
- Crash reports and anonymous daily stats help balance the game.

## V6.4 — 3D Pack Opening
- Pack openings show the **real 3D parts** instead of cards:
  - The foil tears or the crate lid blasts off, and the parts rise out as mystery silhouettes.
  - Each part spins up in a light pillar and bursts into its real colors.
- **Legendary** pulls get a screen shake and a slam; **shiny** parts shimmer in rainbow colors.
- **NEW!** badges, graded IVs on every part and a pity meter ("Legendary guaranteed in N packs").

## V6.3 — Shop Overhaul
- A game-store layout:
  - A weekly featured banner and a daily rotation with a countdown.
  - Tabs for packs, parts, bundles and cosmetics.
- **Gems** come from achievements, gym badges, weekly bosses, raids, streaks and ranked seasons.
- Specific part offers, themed bundles, a wishlist, 3D try-on, and gifting to friends.
- A flashy swipe-to-tear pack opening.

## V6.2 — Fighting-Game Battles
- Fighting-game HUD with portraits, VS splash, a launcher close-up with ripcord meter, and a stats breakdown after every match.

## V6.1 — Home Hub
- Rip-animation splash, Home hub, daily missions, 7-day streak, player card banners, frames and titles.

## V6.0 — New Look
- Hybrid X-treme × Neon theme, bottom dock and 3D packs and crates with odds.
- Accessibility options and unlockable UI themes.

## V5.2 — Adventure
- Adventure mode: a 30-floor Tower, 10 element gyms, a weekly boss and co-op raids.
- Terrain stadiums (lava, ice, storm, void) and Legendary signature moves.

## V5.1 — Own Your Parts
- Every part you own has fixed IVs, plus EVs you assign, part levels and skill trees.
- Packs, crates, shinies and Battle Beys built from your own parts.

## V5.0 — Launch Formations
- Launch formations: pick 1 move per part before every launch.
- Part-based moves, elements with dual typing, and status effects.
- Tap-to-lean, and CPU difficulty levels.

## V4.3 — Real Battles
- Beys hunt each other for way more collisions.
- A burst meter, more burst finishes, and a coin shop.
